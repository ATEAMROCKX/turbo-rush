Turbo Rush – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Turbo Rush is a car racing game made by a young game designer and her family. We care about privacy, especially for kids, so the game is built to collect nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Turbo Rush has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. We do not collect, store or share any personal information.

Game progress

Your coins, cars, garage designs and challenge stars are saved only on your own device. They never leave it. Deleting the app deletes this saved progress.

Game updates

The app may download new versions of the game (new cars, challenges and fixes) from our update service. These downloads do not include any personal information.

Children

Turbo Rush is suitable for children. Because we collect no personal information, no information about children is collected either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above.
