Turbo Rush – Support

Thanks for playing Turbo Rush! 🏎️

How to play

  • Steer: left thumb on ◀ ▶, or touch the left or right side of the road.
  • Brake: hold BRAKE (right thumb). Your car speeds up by itself.
  • Nitro: tap NITRO for a speed boost. Zoom close past cars to refill it.
  • Power-ups: in Races, when the POWER button glows yellow, tap it.
  • 2 Player: each player gets their own half of the screen and their own buttons.

Common questions

I lost my coins! Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Is there anything to buy? No. Turbo Rush is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
