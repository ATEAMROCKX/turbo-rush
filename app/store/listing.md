## Description (4000)
Turbo Rush is a fast, colourful highway racing game designed by a young game maker.

RACE TO WIN
• 15 races across 3 seasons — beat 5 rival racers to the finish line
• Grab power-ups: boost, shield, freeze, oil slick, ram… and DUCK CROSSING, which makes the rivals stop while a family of ducks waddles across the road!
• Smash into rival cars to knock them out of the way

70 CHALLENGES
• Cone slaloms, coin rushes, traffic survival, near misses, perfect stops and more
• Earn up to 3 stars on every level

YOUR DREAM GARAGE
• 8 cars from City Hatch to Rocket
• 33 paints including Lava, Galaxy, Rainbow and Disco
• Decals, neon, spoilers, flames, chrome, a musical horn — and your own name on the number plate

2 PLAYER SPLIT-SCREEN
• Race a friend on one device, each with your own car

SEASONAL SURPRISES
• Spooky Halloween, snowy Christmas and Easter egg themes switch on automatically

Free. No ads. No in-app purchases. No data collected. Works offline.
